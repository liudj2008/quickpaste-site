Questions

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser, using Chrome/Edge extension storage. QuickPaste has no servers that receive your entries. If you use browser sync, your entries sync through your own Google or Microsoft account, never through us.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it and use it. Pro only needs the license key from your purchase email.

What happens if I hit the 10-entry limit?

Nothing is deleted or locked. Your existing entries keep working and you can still edit or delete them. You just can't add an 11th until you delete one or upgrade to Pro.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It's a one-time $9.99 payment with long-term support and updates.

Why does it ask for clipboard permission?

Some sites block ordinary typing and only accept a real paste. QuickPaste briefly uses the clipboard to insert your text, then restores what was there. Your clipboard contents are never stored or sent anywhere.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge (and other Chromium browsers that install from the Chrome Web Store).
