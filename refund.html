Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

We want you to be happy with QuickPaste Pro. If it isn't right for you, you can get a full refund within 30 days of purchase, no questions asked.

How to request a refund

Email support@yltechstudio.com with the email address you used to buy (or your order number). Refunds are returned to your original payment method. Your bank may take a few business days to show it.

After a refund

The license key is deactivated and Pro features turn off. Nothing is deleted: all your saved entries keep working, and you can still edit or delete them. You just can't add new ones beyond the Free limit of 15.

After a refund the key stays deactivated. If you want Pro again later, you can buy it again and you will get a new key.

Free version

The Free version costs nothing, so you can try QuickPaste before you buy.
