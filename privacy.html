Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

QuickPaste is built to be private. In short: your saved text stays on your device, and we don't collect it.

What QuickPaste stores

The entries you save (names and text), your on/off setting, and, if you buy Pro, your license key. All of this is kept in your browser's local extension storage on your own device. We have no server that receives it.

What we do not do

  • No account or login.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data. We don't have your data.
  • No inserting text into password fields, and no reading of password fields.

Permissions and why

  • Access to websites: to detect what you're typing in text fields and show suggestions, and to insert the text you choose. Page content is processed on your device only.
  • Clipboard read/write: some sites only accept a real paste. QuickPaste briefly places your chosen text on the clipboard, pastes it, and restores your previous clipboard. Clipboard contents are never stored or transmitted.
  • Storage: to save your entries locally.

The one network request (Pro license)

When you activate a Pro license key, QuickPaste sends that key to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, to confirm it is valid. It may re-check occasionally. No entries or browsing data are sent. If you never activate Pro, QuickPaste makes no network requests. Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in QuickPaste's settings, or remove the extension to erase everything it stored.
